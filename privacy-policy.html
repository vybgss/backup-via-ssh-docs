Privacy Policy

Your servers, keys and backups stay yours. The app collects nothing.

Last updated: October 24, 2026
No data collection No accounts No analytics or tracking No third parties

This Privacy Policy explains how the macOS app Backup via SSH (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. In short: the app works entirely on your Mac and talks only to the servers you add. We do not collect, receive or have access to any of your data.

1. Information the app stores on your Mac

To do its job, the app keeps the following on your Mac, inside its sandboxed container:

  • Server settings you enter: name, host, user, port, the path to your SSH key, directories to back up, color, icon, schedule and how many archives to keep.
  • Backup history: when each backup ran, how long it took, the archive name and size, and the error message if a backup failed.
  • Folder access: macOS security bookmarks for the folders you grant through the standard Open panel (your .ssh folder and the folder for backups).
  • Preferences such as whether to show the menu bar item and notifications.

This information never leaves your Mac. Deleting a server removes its settings, and a server's history can be cleared from its page at any time.

2. SSH keys and passphrases

  • The app stores only the path to your private key. The key itself stays where it is and is read by the macOS ssh tools; the app never copies or transmits it.
  • For passphrase-protected keys, the key is unlocked in a private ssh-agent that is shut down after each backup.
  • A passphrase is saved only if you tick “Remember in Keychain”. It is then stored in your macOS login Keychain and used solely to unlock that key for backups. You can remove it at any time with Forget in the server's settings; it is also removed when you delete the server.

3. Your backups

Backups are ZIP archives created on your Mac in the folder you choose. They are never uploaded anywhere. Archives you delete in the app, or that fall outside the retention limit you set, are moved to the macOS Trash.

4. Network connections

The only network traffic the app produces is SSH connections, encrypted by SSH, made directly from your Mac to the servers you add. A connection is made only when you start a backup, test a connection, or a schedule you set is due. There are no other servers, intermediaries or background connections.

5. Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal data, usage statistics, crash reports or diagnostics, and it contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. Nothing is sold, shared or disclosed, because nothing is collected. Crash reports, if you choose to share them with developers in macOS settings, are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows local notifications on your Mac when a backup finishes or fails. They are generated on your device and not sent through any server.

7. Exporting your server list

You can export your servers to a JSON file you save yourself. It contains server settings and directory lists, but never keys or passphrases. Because it includes host names and paths, keep exported files somewhere safe.

8. Security

The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox, can only access folders you explicitly grant, and needs only outgoing network access for SSH. You can revoke folder access at any time in Settings → Storage & Access.

9. Children's privacy

The app is a technical utility for people who manage servers. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
